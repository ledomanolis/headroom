Simplified model for discussion only. Liabilities are valued on the chosen funding basis (gilts plus a margin) and projected with random movements in gilt yields, market implied inflation, growth assets and longevity. Growth asset classes are combined using their correlations, but are assumed to move independently of gilt yields and inflation. It does not model realised inflation caps and floors, collateral calls, covenant or detailed insurer pricing. Results need review by a qualified actuary before being relied on.
